# Design Reference — Alliance Technical Group Video

Source: Alliance Technical Group Brand Guidelines v2.1 (Sept 2025) + 2025 Color Palette.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--blue` | `#214592` | Alliance Blue — dominant brand color (CTA scene bg) |
| `--accent` | `#dd6e0c` | Alliance Orange — emphasis + calls-to-action only |
| `--dark` | `#444444` | Alliance Gray |
| `--ink` | `#e8e8e3` | Alliance Light Gray — text on dark, scene 7 bg |
| `--bg` | `#101c33` | Deep navy derived from Alliance Blue (video backdrop) |
| `--blue-bright` | `#3d68c9` | Alliance Blue tint for glows/line art on dark |
| `--muted` | `#8b96ab` | Secondary text on dark |

60-30-10 rule per brand guide: blue-family dominant, light gray secondary, orange as the 10% accent (highlight sweep, rules, stat accents, CTA pill).

## Typography

**Source Sans 3** (brand digital typeface) — 900 for display, 600 for kickers/labels, 300 for body. Weight contrast 300 vs 900.

## Scene map (25s)

1. 0–3s · Hook — "The status quo is slow. Disconnected. Inconsistent."
2. 3–6s · Turn — "There's a better way." (brand promise, orange highlight sweep)
3. 6–9s · Stack Testing (stack line-art draws in)
4. 9–12.5s · CEMS (live signal trace) — shader anchor
5. 12.5–15.5s · Hero — "North America's largest provider of both." — cinematic-zoom reveal
6. 15.5–18.5s · Proof — counters: 25+ years, 2,200+ experts, 60+ locations (from brand guide)
7. 18.5–21.5s · Logo moment on Alliance Light Gray + tagline — light-leak into it
8. 21.5–25s · CTA — Alliance Blue bg, orange pill: www.alliancetg.com — cross-warp-morph
